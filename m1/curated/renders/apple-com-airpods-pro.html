Audio performance

The sound of science.
Powered by the Apple-designed H2 chip, AirPods Pro 3 feature a completely redesigned acoustic architecture that brings breathtaking, all-around-you audio to everything you listen to — from your pump-up playlist to your weekly FaceTime call with family. A new multiport acoustic architecture with more precise airflow control — featuring an inward-facing microphone that points directly into the ear canal — delivers deeper bass, vivid vocal clarity, and a wider soundstage, so you never miss a note. A custom-built driver and amplifier work with the H2 chip to create high-definition, three-dimensional sound, with superlow distortion during playback.

Hearing Protection.

In addition to passive noise reduction from the ear tips, active Hearing Protection helps prevent exposure to loud environmental noise. Machine learning on the H2 chip reduces noise 48,000 times per second while maintaining natural and vibrant sound for whatever you’re listening to.
