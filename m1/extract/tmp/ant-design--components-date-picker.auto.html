Why does the date picker switch to the date panel after selecting the year instead of the month panel?

textAfter selecting the year, the system directly switches to the date panel instead of month panel. This design is intended to reduce the user's operational burden by allowing them to complete the year m

Why config dayjs.locale globally not work?

textWhen you change the layout of nodes by panelRender, React will unmount and re-mount it which reset the component state. You should keep the layout stable. Please ref #27263 for more info.
